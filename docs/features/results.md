# Results

**Who can use this:** School Admin, Teacher, Student  
**Where:** School Admin → Classes (lock/unlock) and Students → Results tab; Teacher → Students → Results tab; Student → Results

## Overview

Results show term grades and remarks for students. Teachers enter scores on the Grading page and submit class results from Classes. School admins lock or unlock results per term. Students view published results for themselves.

## School Admin

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| On **Classes**, select a term and lock or unlock results for one or all classes. | Results for that term become locked or unlocked. Teachers cannot change scores while locked. |
| Open a student’s profile, go to the **Results** tab, and select an academic calendar. | That student’s term results display for the selected calendar. |

## Teacher

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Open a student’s profile from **Students**, go to the **Results** tab, and pick a calendar or term. | The student’s term results display for your review. |

## Student

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Open **Results** and select an academic calendar. | Your term results load for the selected period. |
| Click **Export** when available. | Your results are downloaded or exported. |

## Empty, error, and blocked states

- Teachers see disabled grading when results are submitted or admin-locked (see [grading.md](./grading.md) and [classes.md](./classes.md)).
- The parent **Academics** tab on **Family Dashboard** is temporarily hidden. Old bookmarks to `/parent/results` or `?tab=academics` open **Family Dashboard** on the **Attendance** tab instead.
